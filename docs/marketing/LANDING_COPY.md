## 2. The hook, shown (3 tiles)

**Draw your tone**
Sketch an EQ curve directly on the live scope. What you draw is what you hear —
no menus, no typing numbers.

**Morph the character**
Drag the XY pad to blend six distortion parameters at once. Find the sweet spot
with your hand, not a manual.

**Watch it work**
A real-time oscilloscope shows exactly what the distortion is doing to your
waveform — so you shape with your eyes and ears together.

## Notes for whoever builds the page

- **One field only.** Every extra field (name, "how'd you hear") costs downloads.
- **Deliver instantly.** Show the direct download on the confirmation screen AND
  email it — don't make anyone wait on email to get the file.
- **Lead the page with the GIF**, not text. Audio/visual product = show it.
- **Have an audio player** with 3–4 before/after clips near the toolbox section
  (see DEMO_SCRIPT.md for which presets/sources).
- **GDPR:** if you'll collect EU emails, keep the privacy link real and the
  consent line honest. Extend `docs/PRIVACY.md` to cover the mailing list.
