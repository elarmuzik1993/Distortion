# Demo Content Guide — Video + Audio A/B

The single biggest conversion lever for an audio plugin is **hearing it**. No
producer downloads a distortion they haven't heard. This guide gives you a
shot-by-shot video script and an exact before/after audio plan so you can record
without guessing.

---

## A. The 45–60s launch video (shot-by-shot)

Goal: land the **hook** in the first 5 seconds, prove it's a real tool, end on
"it's free." No talking-head needed — screen capture + the audio does the work.
Add short on-screen text captions (most people watch muted first).

| Time | Visual | Audio | On-screen caption |
|------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| 0:00–0:05 | Cursor **draws an EQ curve on the scope**, tone morphs in real time | A loop playing, tone visibly/audibly changing as you draw | **"Draw your distortion."** |
| 0:05–0:12 | Drag the **XY morph pad** around; knobs move together | Same loop, character morphing from smooth → aggressive | **"Morph it by hand."** |
| 0:12–0:22 | Click through **clip types** on a drum loop | Dry → Drum Smash preset. Big, obvious glow-up | **"7 clip engines."** |
| 0:22–0:32 | Load **Bass Driver** on a bass; point at Sub Guard | Bass dry → driven, low end stays tight | **"Bass stays tight — Sub Guard."** |
| 0:32–0:42 | Quick montage: **Vocal Warmth**, **Screamer**, **Vintage Tape** | 3 fast A/B stabs | **"16 pro presets."** |
| 0:42–0:52 | Show it running in a couple of DAWs / the logo | Best preset riding under the VO/music | **"Win · macOS · Linux"** |
| 0:52–0:60 | Logo + URL + email CTA | Music resolves | **"Free. Get it → [your URL]"** |

**Rules**
- First 5 seconds = the draw-on-scope hook. Nothing else earns attention faster.
- Always show **A/B** (dry vs. processed). The contrast *is* the sell.
- Keep captions on — assume muted autoplay on socials.
- Export a **10–15s vertical cut** (the draw-EQ + one drum A/B) for
  Reels/TikTok/Shorts. That short clip is your top-of-funnel.

**Optional voiceover script (if you narrate):**
> "Most distortion plugins, you dial in numbers. Sledge, you *draw* it — right on
> the scope. Morph the character with the XY pad… then drive it through seven
> analog-style clip engines. Tight low end, sixteen pro presets, runs on
> everything. And it's completely free. Link's below."

## B. Before/After audio clips (for the landing page player)

Record short **dry → processed** pairs, ~4–8 seconds each, same level-matched
loop. Put them in an audio player near the "toolbox" section of the landing page.

| Clip | Source material | Preset | What to listen for |
|------|-----------------|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | Drum bus / breakbeat loop | **Drum Smash** | Parallel grit, punch, glue |
| 2 | Sub/808 or bass guitar | **Bass Driver** | Harmonics added, low end still tight (Sub Guard) |
| 3 | Lead vocal (dry) | **Vocal Warmth** | Subtle analog color, not obviously "distorted" |
| 4 | Guitar or synth lead | **Screamer** | Aggressive, singing sustain |
| 5 (optional) | Full loop / drum machine | **Vintage Tape** | Saturation glue across the mix |

**Recording tips**
- **Level-match** dry vs. wet (use Sledge's auto-gain, then trim by ear). An A/B
  that's just "louder" isn't a fair demo and listeners can tell.
- Keep each clip short and loopable — people scrub, they don't sit through 30s.
- Same loop for dry and wet so the *only* variable is the plugin.
- Bounce to a web-friendly format (MP3/OGG ~192kbps is fine for demos).
- Name files clearly: `sledge-drumsmash-ab.mp3`, etc.

## C. Screenshots / GIF checklist (for landing + forum posts)

- [ ] **Hero GIF:** cursor drawing the EQ curve on the scope, sound morphing (the
      money shot — loop it clean, 3–5s).
- [ ] **XY morph GIF:** dragging the pad, knobs following.
- [ ] Clean full-window screenshot (default preset) — for KVR/BPB listings.
- [ ] One screenshot with a colorful drawn EQ curve — shows the hook as a still.
- [ ] Compact-mode screenshot (scope folded) — shows it's tidy on a busy screen.

Tools that are free: OBS (screen capture), ScreenToGif / Kap (GIFs), Audacity
(clip editing). No budget required for any of this.
