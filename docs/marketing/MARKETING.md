# Sledge Distortion — Positioning & Messaging

Single source of truth for how we talk about Sledge. All landing copy, forum
posts, video scripts, and social captions should ladder up to this.

---

## The hook (locked)

**Sculpt your distortion — don't just dial it in.**

Sledge's identity is **hands-on, visual sound-shaping**: you draw an EQ curve and
morph the character *directly on the live oscilloscope*, instead of hunting
through menus and numeric fields. That's the one thing no other free distortion
does, it's visual, and it demos in 10 seconds.

Everything else — 7 clip types, optical compression, tempo-synced LFO, sub-bass
protection — is the **"…and it's a full studio-grade toolbox underneath"** second
beat. Never lead with the feature list.

### One-liner
> **Sledge Distortion — the free distortion you shape by hand. Draw your tone
> straight on the scope, morph the character with an XY pad, and drive it through
> 7 analog-style clip engines. Windows · macOS · Linux.**

### Elevator (2 sentences)
> Sledge is a free, studio-grade distortion where you *sculpt* the sound instead
> of dialing numbers — draw an EQ curve right on the oscilloscope and morph the
> character with an XY pad. Under the hood it's a serious toolbox: 7 clip
> engines, LA-2A-style compression, a tempo-synced LFO, and sub-bass protection,
> validated in every major DAW on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Why it's free (the honest story — this sells)

Sledge is a first release, given away free in exchange for an email so we can
send you updates and the next plugin. Free-to-use in **any** production,
including commercial releases. That story is a feature: producers love backing an
indie dev early, and "free + this deep" is inherently shareable.

**Always state, on every store/landing page:**
> Free to use in your music, including commercial productions.

(The *source code* is PolyForm Noncommercial — that governs reuse of the code,
**not** use of the plugin. Keep these separate and never let a producer wonder
whether they can release a track made with it. They can.)

## Proof points (translate specs → buyer language)

Lead with the benefit; the spec is the receipt, not the headline.

| Say this (buyer-legible) | Backed by (the receipt) |
|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| "Sounds clean even when driven hard" | 4× oversampling, ISP protection, −0.5 dBFS limiter |
| "Your low end stays tight and powerful" | Sub Guard 50–200 Hz crossover keeps bass out of the distortion |
| "Rock-solid in every DAW, on every OS" | pluginval strictness-10 + auval in CI; Win/macOS/Linux |
| "Won't glitch or crash your session" | RT-safe DSP, 2,460+ automated tests, 24h soak testing |
| "16 pro presets to start from" | Drum Smash, Bass Driver, Vocal Warmth, Screamer, Vintage Tape… |

**Rule:** never put "pluginval strictness 10" or "2,460 assertions" in
front-of-house copy. Translate to "tested in every major DAW" / "won't crash your
session." The raw specs live only here and in a "For the nerds" footnote.

## Audience & tone

- **Who:** bedroom / project-studio producers (hip-hop, electronic, rock, lo-fi),
  Reaper/Ableton/FL/Logic users, and the free-plugin-hunter crowd (BPB readers,
  r/edmproduction).
- **Tone:** confident, plain-spoken, a little gritty — matches "Sledge." No
  corporate fluff, no exclamation-mark spam. Let the sound and the visual hook do
  the talking.

## Naming / SEO note

Always brand it as the two-word unit **"Sledge Distortion"** (never bare
"Sledge" — that collides with the Nord Sledge synth in search). Own the exact
string everywhere: site title, YouTube title, KVR listing, file names.

## Flagship presets to feature (in demos, screenshots, captions)

1. **Drum Smash** — parallel grit on a drum bus (the instant "whoa")
2. **Bass Driver** — thick bass with Sub Guard keeping the low end tight
3. **Vocal Warmth** — subtle analog character on vocals
4. **Screamer** — aggressive lead/guitar tone
5. **Vintage Tape** — saturation glue

## Do / Don't

- ✅ Lead with the draw-on-scope / morph visual. Show, don't spec.
- ✅ Say "free — including commercial use" every time.
- ✅ Anchor trust: "built in the open, checksums provided, unsigned-but-safe."
- ❌ Don't open with a 20-item feature list.
- ❌ Don't bury that it's cross-platform (Linux support is a rare flex — say it).
- ❌ Don't use raw QA jargon in front-of-house copy.
